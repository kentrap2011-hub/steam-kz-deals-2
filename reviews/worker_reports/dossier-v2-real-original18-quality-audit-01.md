## Cross-cutting semantic and provenance QA

**Positive finding:** all 14 saved JSONs were read at their pinned pilot store paths. They contain six to nine substantive, usually game-specific observation statements, explicit strengths **and** player trade-offs, 12 classified coverage dimensions, per-observation feedback joins, temporal statuses, and no obvious pasted raw review bodies or literal author identities. Categories are generally aligned to the corresponding game's distinctive play loop. No evidence of a single generic semantic paragraph cloned across distinct games was found. This supports prospective *utility*, not attested source-by-source truth.

**Important limitations / quality hypotheses:**

1. **Overall strength `limited` in all 14**; no case deserves implicit “representative player consensus.” Every persisted feedback item is a dossier-local `inspected_collection_item` record without an externally stable child review locator. This acquisition mode is **explicitly allowed** by the contract and should not be mislabeled invalid; it nonetheless makes future independent per-review replay/deduplication weaker. Some dates are null. Claims of multiple independent **physical** sources should be rechecked: Steam Community `/reviews/`, `/negativereviews/`, or language-filter variants can be projections of the same platform, not necessarily separate discovery origins.
2. **Coverage is bookkeeping, not quality score.** `covered` can cite the same observation repeatedly across dimensions; unexamined issues marked `not_material_or_not_applicable` are assertions by the researcher. Game-specific known trade-offs sometimes have less breadth than existing live dossiers. In particular solo/coop, late-content, localization, DLC, current platform state and publisher patch chronology merit risk-based checking; do not fabricate coverage to reach 12/12.
3. The identical top-level summary template (“Evidence summary: N validated structured observations...”) in all 14 is a **canonical required projection** from the strict code, **not** proof of generic Research. The observations/conflicts, not that summary, must feed any downstream semantic judgment.
4. `historical` / `current` labels must honor the actual source-role/date contract. At `206420` they demonstrably do not. Other accepted technical complaints are typically `uncertain` and explicitly qualified; do not convert those to 2026 defects. `SULFUR` is a concrete external freshness discontinuity not captured merely by 30-day dossier TTL. Separately track **work original release** versus **Steam AppID/edition release** (Sifu 2022/2023, Sonic Adventure 2 2001/2012, Saints Row IV vs Re-Elected).
5. A successful workflow run tests writer persistence, not semantic accuracy. The missing four files are intentional writer exclusions, not lost records. `invalid` in the log lacks subtype/diagnostic; typed receipts and candidate contents remain independently reviewable, and no recovery is authorized here.
