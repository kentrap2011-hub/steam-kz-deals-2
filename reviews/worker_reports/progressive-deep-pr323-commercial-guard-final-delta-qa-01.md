## Explicit scope limits and residual risk

- `рубл.` is now covered as a conventional distinct abbreviation. A **bare currency glyph** without an amount remains accepted; glyph + amount rejected. `R U B 500` (arbitrary split letters) remains accepted. Ambiguous standalone English `rub` and `Sale` are rejected even in noncommercial meanings; this conservatively favors blocking ambiguous lexical terms. These are explicit policy limits, not permission to invent semantic discrimination in this QA.
- Residual bounded edge cases from the independent corpus: `foo100rub` and `foo100руб` reject because `_currency` permits currency after digits even if those digits are themselves part of a longer identifier. Conversely `рrice100`, `цeна100` and `prіce100` are accepted because a visually folded candidate must fully match the *existing whole-word* patterns and commercial vocabulary glued to a numeral is not covered. None is among the concrete required bypass cases; changing these tradeoffs needs deliberate lexical-policy/false-positive assessment by the owner, **not** an unsanctioned QA change.

The independent reproducer checks the forms separately, and the full CI passes; this is a nonblocking targeted test-coverage cleanup candidate for a future authorized source follow-up, **not** a justification to edit PR #323 from QA.
