## Implementation and self-review
- Concurrency-safe `_SharedAppdetailsPacer`: under one Condition/Lock, prune expired request starts and reserve a new timestamp only if fewer than **180 starts** remain in half-open moving `(now - 300s, now]`. At exactly t=300 a t=0 start expires. Hold lock for checking/reserving, **never** across waiting or HTTP GET. The same instance is used by all parent and child lookups and every 429 retry. Preserve existing >=0.9-second spacing and shared 429 cooldown. FIFO waiter tickets prevent three threads from overtaking the head; followers sleep on Condition without polling and are notified when a head reserves, cancels or times out; every wake rechecks the window and cooldown.
- `180/300s` is an internal conservative process-local safety bound chosen below observed first live 429 near 202 starts/300s, **not** an official Valve quota or proof about shared outbound IP traffic.
- Old `APPDETAILS_MAX_WAIT_SECONDS=120` now bounds cumulative **per-lookup own 429 cooldown** only; legitimate rolling-window waiting does not consume it. A new **900-second per-lookup overall deadline** bounds planned slot waiting (network individual GET timeout remains 25s; full GitHub `collect` job remains capped at 60min). Retry-After remains capped at 60s per event, fallback 5/15/30s, max four attempts, persistent 429 fails closed. 403, 5xx, network exceptions, malformed/missing/wrong AppIDs still fail closed; no fake data, partial accepted outcome or bypass.
- Identity/scope unchanged: exact `cc=kz&l=english`, `steam_appid`, parent `type=game` and deduplicated relation IDs, child `type=dlc`, verified `fullgame`, KZT price, discount threshold and complete lookup count. Existing passive per-request diagnostics retained; the pacer reservation timestamp is reused as telemetry start time.
- Self-review: atomic lock protects both deque and queue/head ticket; at most the 180th append can succeed until oldest slot exits the half-open 300s window. The FIFO head cannot be overtaken and on exception its ticket is removed and followers notified. 429-triggered cooldown extends the shared gate, never creates a free slot. No global DLC browsing or reduced parent/child coverage. The physical HTTP call immediately follows its reserved admission; network/IP-wide Valve quota cannot be proven by process-local admission alone.
