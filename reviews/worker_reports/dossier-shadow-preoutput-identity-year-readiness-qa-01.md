### Concrete positive release-year conflict candidates — escalate to Director

1. **AppID 213610 — Sonic Adventure 2**, legacy Dossier *original-work year claim* **2012**. Publisher SEGA's 2001 Dreamcast original product page clearly documents **23 June 2001**: https://sonic.sega.jp/sonicadv2/sonic_e.html ; exact Steam AppID page gives **19 November 2012**: https://store.steampowered.com/app/213610/ . **Original work 2001 vs Steam product 2012**. This is same underlying game on a later platform, not proof that the underlying game debuted in 2012.
2. **AppID 2138710 — Sifu**, legacy **2023**. Developer Sloclap records original **8 February 2022** PlayStation/Epic release at https://www.sifugame.com/news/sifu-is-available-now ; its official developer news documents arrival on Steam **28 March 2023** at https://www.sifugame.com/news and the linked Steam listing https://store.steampowered.com/app/2138710/ . Original 2022, Steam 2023, same work.
3. **AppID 1638220 — Xuan-Yuan Sword: The Scar of Sky**, legacy **2021**. Independently attributed per-platform historical archive places original Windows/Taiwan release at **6 December 2000**, https://www.mobygames.com/game/19354/xuan-yuan-sword-the-scar-of-sky/releases/ ; exact Steam Store https://store.steampowered.com/app/1638220/ displays **18 August 2021**. **Original work 2000 vs Steam 2021**. Source is attributed secondary historical catalog, not publisher's original 2000 press release; recheck any day-level historic discrepancy before final approval.

These are positive **candidates for an incorrect old original/work-year binding**, **not** a retrospective alteration of already writer-accepted Dossier records or a declaration that the old strict group acceptance was invalid.
